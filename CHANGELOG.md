# Changelog

All updates are included with your UBClaude licence.

## 1.4.0 — November 2026
- New name: Cub is now **UBClaude**. Your settings, trial and licence carry over.

## 1.3.1 — November 2026
- One-click activation: the button in your purchase email opens UBClaude already activated.

## 1.3.0 — November 2026
- Free for 24 hours, then activate with the key from your purchase email.
- One key works on up to 3 Macs; move it from Settings → Licence.
- Works offline after activation.

## 1.2.0 — November 2026
- New welcome screen: detects whether Claude Code is installed and signed in, and walks you through it.
- Connects automatically the moment you sign in, no restart needed.
- One-click reconnect when your Claude Code login expires.

## 1.1.1 — November 2026
- Panel stays put when switching tabs.
- Instant refresh button (⌘R).
- Windows that already reset show 0% right away.

## 1.1.0 — November 2026
- Tokens tab: today, 7 days, this month, 14-day chart, per-model breakdown.
- Pace forecast for every limit.
- Per-model weekly and monthly extra-usage bars.
- Session-reset notification and menu bar display options.
