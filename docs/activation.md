# Free trial and activation

## The free trial
UBClaude is **free for 24 hours** from the first launch, with every feature. A strip at the top of the panel shows the time left.

## Buy a licence
After the trial, UBClaude locks and asks for an activation key. Buy a lifetime licence for **$9 once** at [usagebarclaude.com](https://usagebarclaude.com/#pricing) (card, Apple Pay or Google Pay).

## Activate in one click
Right after paying you receive an email from **contact@usagebarclaude.com** with:
- your activation key (`CUB-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`), and
- an **Activate UBClaude in one click** button: it opens UBClaude already activated.

## Activate by hand
Click UBClaude in the menu bar → paste the key → **Activate**.

## Use it on another Mac
One key works on **up to 3 Macs**. To move it, open **Settings → Licence → Deactivate this Mac** on the old one, then activate on the new one.

## Offline
After activation UBClaude works offline. It checks the licence again every few days when it's online.

## Lost your key?
Email **contact@usagebarclaude.com** from the address you paid with.

## Refunds
Not happy? Email us within 14 days for a full refund. Your key is cancelled when the refund is made. See the [refund policy](https://usagebarclaude.com/refund).
