# Installation

## Requirements
- macOS 13 Ventura or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel Mac (UBClaude is a universal app)
- [Claude Code](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/claude-code) signed in with a Claude Pro or Max plan

## Install
1. Download **UBClaude.dmg** from the [latest release](../../../releases/latest) or from [usagebarclaude.com](https://usagebarclaude.com).
2. Open the DMG and drag **UBClaude** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open UBClaude from Applications. It appears in the menu bar, next to the clock (a small ring icon).

## "macOS can't check this app"
If macOS says the app can't be opened because Apple can't check it:
1. Open **Applications**, right-click **UBClaude** and choose **Open**, then **Open** again; or
2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

You only need to do this once.

## Update
Download the latest DMG and replace the app in Applications. Your settings, trial and licence are kept.

## Uninstall
Quit UBClaude (panel → **Quit UBClaude**), then move it from Applications to the Trash.
If you want to use your licence on another Mac, deactivate it first in **Settings → Licence**.
